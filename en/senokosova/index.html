Dance dramaturg for “Archaeology of the Human”

Anastasia
Senokosova

Choreographer · Contemporary dance teacher · Dance dramaturg

Trained professionally in dance in Cyprus — at the international contemporary dance school Pera School of Performing Arts at Girne American University — and completed the PART Academy program at the St. Petersburg Institute of Film and Television.

Before dance, she graduated from Bauman Moscow State Technical University with an engineering degree — it shows in how she works with structure, detail, and the internal logic of movement. She works as an independent teacher, with a practice spanning contemporary dance, free movement, and improvisation.

At “Koroche” she handles the dance dramaturgy of "Gender" as part of the evening "Archaeology of the Human" — a program under the artistic direction of Yulia Bychkova.
