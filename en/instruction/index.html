About the project

{ 01 / CONCEPT }

A joint project by dance companies “Koroche” and “Zabava”, a flagship of Kaluga's dance scene. A show about the road, about choice, and about what it means to begin — for those already on the road, those partway there, and those about to set out. Co-directed by Yulia Bychkova (“Koroche”) and Elena Inozemtseva (“Zabava”).
