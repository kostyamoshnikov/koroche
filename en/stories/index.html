About the project

{ 01 / CONCEPT }

A physical theatre piece weaving together lightness and weight, tradition and modernity, reality and metaphor. “Stories” marked the company's first tour to St. Petersburg — the show played to a warm reception at the New Stage of the Alexandrinsky Theatre.
