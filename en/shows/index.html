About the project

{ 02 / CONCEPT }

"Archaeology of the Human" is an excavation — except instead of a spade, there's the body. Three choreographers dig through different layers: gender and origin, power and submission, the body's memory and its recurring gesture.

Each part is a complete statement with its own author and cast. Together they form one evening about what remains human once every role is taken off.
