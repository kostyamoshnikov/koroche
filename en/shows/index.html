About the project

{ 02 / CONCEPT }

“Archaeology of the Human” is an excavation — except the shovel is the body. Three choreographers dig through different layers: sex and origin, power and submission, the body's memory and a repeated gesture.

Each part is a complete statement with its own author and cast. Together they form one evening about what remains human once every role is stripped away.
