About

{ 01 / ABOUT US }

Yulia Bychkova founded the company in 2020. Since then the repertoire has grown to more than five productions — all made in the language of contemporary dance, where the body speaks without translation.

A young, fast-growing company, {koroche} recently took its first tour to St. Petersburg: the show "Stories. Of the Earthly and the Heavenly" played to a warm reception at the New Stage of the Alexandrinsky Theatre.

{koroche} recently premiered "Archaeology of the Human" — an evening of short-form contemporary dance in three independent statements.
